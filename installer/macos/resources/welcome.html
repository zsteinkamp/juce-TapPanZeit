TapPanZeit

A multitap (up to 128) delay with drawable control over each tap's timing, pan position, and volume.

This installs the VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins into your system plug-in folders. Click Customize on the next screen to choose just one.

After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW. TapPanZeit is listed under Zack Steinkamp.
